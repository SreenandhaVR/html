Accesbility

Accesibility means making message usable bt as many people as possible.While it mostly focuses on supporting people with disabilities,this practice benefuts everyone.Even if you don't have a disability.We might be browsing on an mobile device with a smaller screen or using a slow internet connection.Accessible techniques can improve the experience for everyone in these situations as well.The core principle is simple:Everyone should be able to access to the web and in some places it's actually the law.The w3c has a complete guide to accessibility laws and policies by country.The legal requirements vary by region,but they usually apply to government agencies and sectors such as banking,telecommunications,libraries,educational institutions and transportation.The W#C has also created the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines,which outline the best practices for making web content accessible.This guide is extensive and has lot of detailed information,so I recommend starting hte WCAG At a GLance article.
